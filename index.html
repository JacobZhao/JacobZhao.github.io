About me

I am a Specially Appointed Assistant Professor at The University of Osaka, working in the Information Systems Synthesis Laboratory.

My research connects artificial intelligence, mathematical optimization, and real-world energy systems. I develop data-driven models and control methods that make buildings and distributed energy resources more efficient, flexible, and responsive to people's needs.

My work spans HVAC energy management, demand-side flexibility, battery scheduling, and renewable energy integration, with an emphasis on interpretable models and experimental validation. I am also exploring how physically constrained agentic AI can support energy scheduling.

I received my Ph.D. in Information Science from Osaka University in 2022 and my master's degree from Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST).

Research interests

Smart buildings & HVAC

Data-driven thermal modeling, model predictive control, and comfort-aware operation in real buildings.

Demand-side flexibility

Coordinating flexible loads through reinforcement learning, optimization, and hierarchical aggregation.

Storage & renewable energy

Degradation-aware battery scheduling, PV integration, and energy management across multiple timescales.

AI for energy systems

Symbolic regression, forecasting, physics-informed models, and agentic AI with physical constraints.
